## 🌐 Sector Overview & Market Dynamics

The global **Wireless Network Security and Auditing market** is estimated at **$18.5 Billion in 2026** (growing at a compound annual growth rate of ~14.2% driven by enterprise Wi-Fi 6E/7 upgrades, IoT edge expansions, and stringent zero-trust regulatory mandates). The sector is **moderately fragmented**: enterprise infrastructure management and automated WIPS containment are concentrated around large public network security giants (such as Cisco Meraki, Tenable, Qualys, and Rapid7), while specialized RF spectrum analysis, deep packet analysis, and offensive penetration-testing suites remain distributed among agile specialized vendors (such as Ekahau, Hak5, NetSpot, and Acrylic Wi-Fi) alongside a vibrant open-source developer ecosystem.

3. Update `README.md` with accurate links, starting tier pricing, free limits, and official documentation.
4. For additional curated technology indexes, check out [Awesome Awesome Awesome](https://github.com/ishandutta2007/Awesome-Awesome-Awesome).
